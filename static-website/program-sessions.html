Explore Our Four Thematic Tracks

Click any card to see the track's full session, moderator & speakers

Design AI

Theme: Design Thinking in the Age of Generative AI

AI is rapidly reshaping how we understand, practise and teach design thinking, opening new possibilities while challenging established ideas of creativity, expertise and human agency. Bringing together perspectives from the global south and north, this panel explores how different design communities are responding to the rise of AI and what it means for the future of human-centred innovation.

Moderator: Ms. Raika Rauch

View Track & Speakers

Social Impact

Theme: Design Thinking as a Catalyst for Community Change

Addressing complex social challenges requires more than good ideas — it calls for an understanding of systems, communities and the different realities in which change takes place. Bringing together perspectives from the global south and north, this panel explores how Design Thinking can contribute to meaningful social impact across diverse cultural and societal contexts.

Moderator: Richard Perez

View Track & Speakers

Education Transformation

Theme: From the Teaching Side to the Learning Side

Education is shifting from knowledge transfer to self-directed, collaborative and challenge-based learning. As AI makes knowledge increasingly accessible, the focus moves toward Future Skills such as creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, agency and the ability to navigate complexity. This panel brings together perspectives from government, teacher education, design, research and the student experience to explore how Design Thinking and AI can help reshape education for the future.

Moderator: Prof. Uli Weinberg

View Track & Speakers

Industry Innovation

Theme: Reimagining Value Creation Through Design Thinking

Industries across the world are being reshaped by new technologies, changing expectations and increasingly complex markets. This panel brings together perspectives from India and the United States to explore how Design Thinking can help organisations rethink products, services, business models and the ways value is created. The discussion considers how different market contexts and approaches to innovation can offer new perspectives on turning uncertainty into opportunity and creating meaningful, sustainable progress.

Moderator: Nalin SNS
