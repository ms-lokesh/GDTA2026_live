About SNS

  1. Home
  2. » About SNS

SNS Group is one of South India's most dynamic multi-sector conglomerates, anchored by Sri SNS Charitable Trust, a philanthropic organisation founded in 1997. Headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, the group operates at the intersection of education, technology, entrepreneurship, real estate, and venture capital — unified by a singular philosophy: building an entrepreneurial mindset through GenAI-powered Design Thinking.

From a single engineering college in 1997 to a sprawling ecosystem of 9 institutions, a technology company, a startup incubator targeting 1,000 AI ventures, and a portfolio of digital platforms, SNS Group has grown into an institution-building powerhouse that shapes over 16,000 students annually and maintains a global alumni network of 40,000+ across 105+ countries.

SNS is India's first institution to implement a patented GenAI-powered Design Thinking Framework across K-12 through PhD education — integrating purpose, process, and people into every aspect of learning and institutional culture.

Vision

To become an internationally recognised ecosystem that produces entrepreneurial, empathetic, and technologically fluent leaders who transform industries and serve society.

Mission

To redesign the common mind and elevate business excellence by embedding Design Thinking, AI literacy, and 3P Culture into every dimension of education, research, and enterprise.

Core Philosophy — 3P Culture
  • Purpose: Every venture and curriculum begins with a compelling 'why' — a north star that guides decisions and galvanises teams.
  • Process: Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test & Scale — a human-centred design cycle applied to education and business alike.
  • People: Mentors, investors, educators, and entrepreneurs united in a shared commitment to excellence and social good.
3C Competency Circle
  • Attitude: Curiosity & accountability; growth mindset cultivated through design thinking practices.
  • Technical: Domain expertise powered by AI & emerging technologies — from GenAI to core engineering.
  • Logical & Business: Critical thinking, problem-solving, and business acumen for real-world success.
