The GDTA India Chapter is proud to host the D Conference 2026, bringing together design thinkers, innovators, and change-makers from across the globe.

This two-day conference will explore how design thinking can navigate chaos to create clarity, driving meaningful progress in organizations, communities, and society.

Experience engaging sessions, hands-on workshops, design safaris, and networking opportunities with leading practitioners.
