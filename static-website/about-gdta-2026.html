About GDTA Conference 2026

  1. Home
  2. » About GDTA Conference 2026

In an increasingly complex world filled with uncertainty, organizations and individuals face unprecedented challenges. The D Conference 2026 explores how design thinking provides a framework to navigate chaos and create clarity.

Through human-centered approaches, collaborative methods, and iterative processes, design thinking helps transform ambiguity into actionable insights and meaningful solutions.

Join us to explore methodologies, share experiences, and build capabilities that drive progress amidst complexity.

Conference Objectives

What We Aim to Achieve

Knowledge Sharing

Exchange best practices, case studies, and insights from design thinking practitioners across diverse industries and contexts.

Capability Building

Develop practical skills through hands-on workshops and experiential learning opportunities.

Community Building

Foster connections, collaborations, and lasting relationships within the design thinking community.
