GDTA Conference 2026: Panel Session Preparation

We are living through a decade of compounding complexity. Technology, climate, and social change are reshaping the ground beneath organisations faster than most can respond — and in that noise, clarity has become the rarest and most valuable resource of all. What do we do when the world refuses to behave like a well-defined problem? As technology accelerates, expectations shift and old assumptions stop holding, the challenge is no longer simply to find answers—but to make sense of complexity and turn uncertainty into purposeful action.

d.Conference 2026 asks a simple question with far-reaching implications: how do we use Design Thinking to navigate chaos and create clarity? Over two days at SNS Institutions in Coimbatore, the global Design Thinking community comes together to turn complexity into direction — sharing what works, testing new ideas, and building the capabilities that help organisations, communities, and societies make meaningful progress. All Panels include at least one voice from the Indian context for local connection and are overall an international group to cover diverse, global perspectives.

Parallel Panels

Parallel Panel Sessions

Concurrent panel discussions exploring key design thinking topics and AI innovation:

Panel 1 - Education Transformation - From the Teaching Side to the Learning Side

Education is shifting from knowledge transfer to self-directed, collaborative and challenge-based learning. As AI makes knowledge increasingly accessible, the focus moves toward Future Skills such as creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, agency and the ability to navigate complexity. This panel brings together perspectives from government, teacher education, design, research and the student experience to explore how Design Thinking and AI can help reshape education for the future.

Agnes Kwek Andre Bresges (Online) Shirley Matthew Hoda Mostafa 1 SNS Student Moderation: Uli Weinberg
Panel 2 - Industry Innovations - Reimagining Value Creation Through Design Thinking

Industries across the world are being reshaped by new technologies, changing expectations and increasingly complex markets. This panel brings together perspectives from India and the United States to explore how Design Thinking can help organisations rethink products, services, business models and the ways value is created. The discussion considers how different market contexts and approaches to innovation can offer new perspectives on turning uncertainty into opportunity and creating meaningful, sustainable progress.

Sreedevi Hegde Revathi Kant Ramesh Ramanujam Matt Parkinson (confirmation pending) Moderation: Nalin S. Kumar
Panel 3 - Design & AI - Design Thinking in the Age of Generative AI

AI is rapidly reshaping how we understand, practise and teach design thinking, opening new possibilities while challenging established ideas of creativity, expertise and human agency. Bringing together perspectives from the global south and north, this panel explores how different design communities are responding to the rise of AI and what it means for the future of human-centred innovation. The discussion considers how we can harness AI’s potential while keeping human judgement, creativity and responsibility at the centre.

Ricardo Ruffo Jacob Reeves Sayantan Mukherjee Moderation: Monika Frech
Panel 4 - Social Impact – Design Thinking as a Catalyst for Community Change

Addressing complex social challenges requires more than good ideas — it calls for an understanding of systems, communities and the different realities in which change takes place. Bringing together perspectives from the global south and north, this panel explores how Design Thinking can contribute to meaningful social impact across diverse cultural and societal contexts. The discussion considers how human-centred approaches can help navigate complexity, build inclusive solutions and turn local insights into lasting change.

Carolin Tänzer Avinash Pratap Singh Tita Larasati (to be confirmed) Moderation: Richard Perez

Workshops

Parallel Workshops

Interactive hands-on workshop tracks exploring cutting-edge Design Thinking techniques:

Workshop 1: Playing the Future: How to Apply Trends for Generating Breakthrough Ideas

This hands-on workshop introduces participants to trend-watching models and practices, connecting them directly with the Design Thinking process and the idea generation phase. Through an interactive tabletop game – "Playing the Future" – teams collaborate to generate innovative ideas for products, services, and markets. The session explores how trends can create new opportunities, illustrated by real-world cases (such as Miro interactive boards), and culminates in team presentations of breakthrough concepts.

Maria Stashenko
Workshop 2: Interactive Innovation Workshop

Hands-on collaborative workshop focusing on modern design thinking methods and practice.

Tebogo Chaka
Workshop 3: Reimagining Design Thinking with Indigenous Knowledge and AI

This interactive workshop invites participants to explore how Design Thinking can be reimagined through the dialogue between Indigenous / traditional knowledge, artificial intelligence, and responsible future-making. Rather than approaching Design Thinking only as a contemporary innovation method, the session will ask how long-standing cultural wisdom, local problem-solving practices, and intergenerational perspectives can help us rethink the way we frame challenges, generate ideas, and imagine futures. Through a simple AI-supported activity, participants will create and compare visual scenarios, reflecting on how the questions we ask shape the futures we design. The workshop aims to open a space for responsible, resilient, and regenerative ways of thinking across design, education, innovation, and society.
