Solving problems that matter.

The digital age has brought us endless tools but often leaves us disconnected from real human needs. GDTA Build4Change is a call to students who want to look beyond the code. We believe innovation should serve the community, not just the market.

Through meaningful narratives and real world challenges, we invite you to design solutions that breathe life into education, urban living, and healthcare. This is not just a competition; it is a movement toward intentional, human centered engineering.
