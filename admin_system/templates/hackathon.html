About the Challenge

GDTA Challenge 2026

The GDTA Challenge 2026 is a global innovation hackathon organized as part of the GDTA Conference 2026. The challenge invites developers, researchers, students, and innovators to build impactful AI-powered solutions addressing critical challenges across industries.

Participants will design intelligent systems capable of analyzing real-world problems and delivering scalable digital solutions that can create meaningful impact in society.

This challenge encourages innovation that combines advanced technologies, responsible AI practices, and practical implementation.

Our Mission

Hackathon Goal

Build intelligent systems that can analyze, reason, and solve real-world problems in sectors that directly impact society.

Participants are encouraged to develop solutions that demonstrate:

  • Innovative use of Artificial Intelligence
  • Scalable and deployable system architecture
  • Multi-modal or data-driven intelligence
  • Integration with real-world tools or datasets
  • Meaningful impact in healthcare, education, or sustainability

This challenge focuses on building practical solutions rather than theoretical prototypes.

Requirements

The Challenge

Participants are expected to develop solutions that demonstrate:

Intelligent System Architecture

Design modular and scalable systems capable of solving complex problems.

Real-Time Data Processing

Utilize modern AI and computing technologies to process information efficiently.

Integration with External Systems

Connect your solution with APIs, datasets, or digital platforms to demonstrate real-world applicability.

Multi-Modal Capabilities

Combine multiple forms of input such as text, data, images, or voice.

Genuine Impact

Address a real-world problem that affects communities, industries, or the environment.

Focus Areas

Challenge Tracks

Healthcare Innovation

Develop AI-powered systems to improve healthcare accessibility, diagnostics, patient monitoring, or healthcare data management.

Example areas:
  • AI-assisted diagnosis
  • Patient monitoring systems
  • Medical data analysis
  • Digital health platforms

EdTech Transformation

Create intelligent systems that enhance education, learning accessibility, and knowledge delivery.

Example areas:
  • Personalized learning systems
  • AI tutoring assistants
  • Skill development platforms
  • Educational analytics

Sustainability & Climate

Design technologies that help monitor environmental impact, optimize resources, and support sustainable development.

Example areas:
  • Environmental monitoring systems
  • Smart energy optimization
  • Waste management solutions
  • Climate data analysis

Final Deliverables

Submission Requirements

Participants must submit:

GitHub Repository

The complete project code

Demonstration Video

A maximum 3-minute demonstration video

Live Deployment

A live deployment link or detailed setup instructions

Documentation should include:

  • Executive Summary: Overview of the problem and solution
  • System Architecture: Design explanation and technical workflow
  • Technical Implementation: Key components, technologies, and integrations
  • Live Demonstration: Complete user journey demonstration
  • Performance & Impact Analysis: Expected benefits and scalability potential
  • Setup & Deployment Guide: Instructions for running the solution
